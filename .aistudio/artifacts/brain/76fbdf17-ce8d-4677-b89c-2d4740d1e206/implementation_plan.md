# Realm of Eldoria: Infinite D&D Adventure

An interactive single-player Dungeons & Dragons web application where an AI Dungeon Master guides the player through an unending campaign across ancient ruins, mythical wilderness, and dangerous dungeons. Players craft a hero from core race and class combinations, roll animated polyhedral dice for ability checks and combat encounters, choose from contextual DM action prompts or type custom maneuvers, and level up with persistent loot, spells, and quests.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The following architectural and design decisions have been confirmed through Phase 1 clarification and are locked into the plan:

- **Confirmed Campaign Setting**: Classic High Fantasy — ancient Elven ruins, primordial wilderness, shadowy crypts, and welcoming frontier taverns in the realm of *Eldoria*.
- **Confirmed Rules & Dice System**: Interactive animated 3D/physics-styled dice rolls (d20 + modifiers) with standard D&D 5e mechanics (Hit Points, Armor Class, Ability Scores: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA, Advantage/Disadvantage, and DC checks).
- **Confirmed Visual Aesthetic**: Minimalist modern fantasy pairing warm charcoal slate surfaces, deep oak/walnut wood textures, gold-leaf accents, clean typography (Cinzel & Plus Jakarta Sans), and zero-pill metadata layouts.
- **Dungeon Master Narration**: Rich, atmospheric second-person prose ("You step into the moss-covered vestibule...") featuring structured turn outcomes, 3–4 tailored action choices, plus an unrestricted custom action input that the DM dynamically interprets.
- **Persistence Strategy**: LocalStorage-backed state engine saving character sheets, quest log, inventory items (weapons, armor, potions, wondrous items), journal entries, and adventure history with export/import capabilities.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Delivers an authentic tabletop RPG experience solo. The player selects or customizes a character (Race + Class + Background), embarks on an episodic, unending fantasy campaign, faces tactical encounters and moral dilemmas, makes d20 rolls against Difficulty Classes (DCs), and manages inventory and character progression (XP, levels, and spell slots/hit dice).
- **Target Audience / Persona**: D&D 5e players, solo adventurers, tabletop RPG enthusiasts, and fantasy fiction fans who want spontaneous, high-quality DM storytelling on demand without scheduling conflicts.
- **Key Value**: Endless sandbox reactivity. Every action—whether chosen from the DM's suggestions or freely typed by the player—causes the world to evolve realistically with authentic 5e rules, persistent consequences, and tactile dice rolling.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows

1. **Character Creation (The Adventurer's Ledger)**:
   - Choose from classic races (Human, High Elf, Mountain Dwarf, Lightfoot Halfling, Tiefling) and classes (Fighter, Wizard, Rogue, Cleric, Ranger, Barbarian).
   - View starting stats, proficiencies, racial traits, starting equipment, and select an adventure moniker.
   - Quick "Begin Journey" button instantly drops the hero into the opening scene at the *Whispering Pines Outpost*.

2. **The Adventure View (Main Play Loop)**:
   - **Chronicle Stream**: Central scrollable narrative feed with DM scene descriptions, dialogue, sensory environmental cues, and historical turns.
   - **Tactical Context Header**: Current location, weather/time, active quest goal, and danger level.
   - **Action Bar**: 3 to 4 distinct preselected options generated by the DM (e.g., *[Perception] Search the altar for glyphs*, *[Athletics] Force open the rusted iron portcullis*, *[Stealth] Slip into the shadows along the cavern wall*).
   - **Custom Action Input**: Text area with a prompt placeholder (*"Declare your own action or spell..."*) and instant submit (`Enter` or `"Take Action"` button).

3. **Dice Rolling & Resolution Modal / Tray**:
   - When an action triggers a check, attack, or saving throw, an interactive dice tray slides into focus.
   - The player rolls a physical 3D/animated d20 (click or drag to roll, with bounce dynamics and sound FX via Web Audio API).
   - Instant calculation: Base roll + Ability Modifier + Proficiency Bonus vs. Target DC, declaring *Critical Hit*, *Success*, *Failure*, or *Critical Fumble*.
   - Results are automatically fed to the DM backend to generate the following narrative beat and consequences.

4. **Character Sheet & Inventory Drawer**:
   - Collapsible side panel or tabbed drawer displaying active HP, Armor Class, Speed, Initiative, Level, XP bar, Ability Modifiers, Inventory with item weights/rarities, Gold/Silver pouches, and active quests.
   - Interactive item use (drinking a Potion of Healing restores HP directly on the sheet; equipping weapons updates attack modifiers).

5. **Resting & Campfire Phase**:
   - Take a *Short Rest* (spend Hit Dice to regain health) or *Long Rest* (recover spell slots, full HP, and trigger DM night encounter or dream sequence).

### Visual Identity & Theme

- **Aesthetic Direction**: Minimalist Modern Fantasy. Clean, architectural layout grounded in tabletop elegance. High readability, generous margins, subtle wood textures, and refined metallic accents.
- **Color Palette (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  - *60% Dominant Neutral Canvas*: Deep midnight slate (`#0B0F17`) and dark parchment charcoal (`#121824`).
  - *30% Structural Surfaces*: Warm aged walnut timber panels (`#1A2232`), hairline bronze borders (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.18)`), clean dark cards (`#151C2C`).
  - *10% Focal Accents*: Antique gilding gold (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C07B`) for primary actions, critical successes, and active tab states; crimson ruby (`#EF4444`) for damage/HP danger; emerald moss (`#10B981`) for healing and success.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**:
  - *Display / Headings*: `Cinzel` serif for chapter titles, location names, and character titles.
  - *Body Prose & Narration*: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Source Serif 4` (16px, 1.65 line-height) for comfortable long-form reading.
  - *Numerals & Stats*: `JetBrains Mono` / `tabular-nums` for dice rolls, modifier calculations, HP values, and gold counts.
- **Zero-Pill Discipline**: All status indicators, room conditions, and item metadata appear as clean, unboxed text separated by subtle bullets (`Ruins of Kaer Mor · Danger: High · Daylight`).
- **Interactive Feedback & Motion**:
  - Smooth 3D roll physics for polyhedral dice using CSS 3D transforms / canvas.
  - Micro-haptic web audio chimes for dice landing, critical roll fanfares, page turn rustles, and sword clash audio cues.
  - Smooth narrative reveal with non-blocking streaming or phased text reveal.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

### Decision 1: AI Model & Architecture — Server-Side Gemini 3.8 Flash
- **Chosen Approach**: Use `@google/genai` on the Express backend (`gemini-3.8-flash`) with structured JSON schemas (`responseSchema`) for predictable game state parsing alongside rich prose.
- **Why**: `gemini-3.8-flash` delivers near-instant response latency (<1.5s), allowing fluent turn-by-turn storytelling without breaking the game flow. Structured outputs ensure the server receives the narrative text, updated location, suggested actions with associated skill check types, loot drops, and XP rewards reliably.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Client-side API calls (violates security & environment constraints); `gemini-4-argon` (higher latency and cost for rapid turn-based gameplay).

### Decision 2: Rule Enforcement & Hybrid Resolution Engine
- **Chosen Approach**: Client-side authoritative 5e math engine combined with DM narrative orchestration. When an action requires a roll, the game determines the relevant ability score (e.g. Dexterity for Acrobatics) and target DC. The user rolls the physical d20 in the UI; the final number + modifiers are sent back to the DM to craft the specific narrative consequence.
- **Why**: Gives the player full tactile agency and transparency. The player sees the dice roll, understands why they passed or failed, and cannot be cheated by a black-box AI.
- **Alternatives Considered**: AI rolling dice invisibly (feels scripted and deprives the player of the core D&D thrill).

### Decision 3: Infinite Campaign Architecture & Context Management
- **Chosen Approach**: Sliding-window narrative buffer + persistent episodic memory ledger. The client maintains:
  1. *Core Character State* (Level, Class, HP, Equipment, Stats).
  2. *World State* (Current Region, Active Quest, Major NPCs Met, Key Deeds).
  3. *Recent Chronicle Buffer* (Last 6–8 dialogue turns for immediate narrative continuity).
  4. *Campaign Journal* (Summarized milestones generated every 10 turns to avoid context token bloat).
- **Why**: Allows the campaign to literally never end while keeping LLM context concise, fast, and coherent across tens of hours of gameplay.

### Interactive Component & State Mapping

1. **`CharacterCreatorModal`**:
   - Interactive race & class selection cards with ability score bonuses preview.
   - Initial gear preview and "Embark into the Unknown" button.
   - Initial state initialization: seeds starting inventory, weapon, armor, and default backstory.

2. **`ChronicleStream`**:
   - Renders chronological narrative turns with subtle typography distinctions between DM narration, NPC dialogue (italicized quotes), and player decisions.
   - Auto-scrolls gracefully to the latest turn upon resolution.

3. **`ActionConsole`**:
   - Renders 3–4 preselected buttons showing required checks (e.g. `[Wisdom (Perception) DC 12] Scan the shadows`).
   - Clicking a check-based option opens the `DiceTrayModal` with the appropriate bonus pre-calculated.
   - Clicking a passive option executes the turn immediately.
   - Text input field with character limit and validation for custom actions.

4. **`DiceTrayModal`**:
   - Animated 3D d20 die rendered using CSS 3D perspectives or canvas.
   - "Click or Press Space to Roll" affordance.
   - Sound synthesis using Web Audio API (realistic click-clack tumble and impact chime).
   - Shows step-by-step breakdown: `Natural Roll: 14 + DEX (+3) + Proficiency (+2) = 19 vs DC 14 (SUCCESS!)`.
   - On completion, dispatches the roll outcome to the DM turn handler.

5. **`InventoryDrawer`**:
   - Categorized equipment list with equip/use buttons.
   - Equipping armor updates AC dynamically; drinking potions triggers HP restoration and floating green healing numbers.
